Hamburg's special days

One special day at a time, the most important first: a quiet day keeps the picture calm, then a birthday, then single days, then periods, then the bin lorry. Holidays come from the date; the rest from your calendars, by keyword.

    See the calendar days at home

    1. Download the demo calendar: one Hamburg event a day, starting tomorrow.
    2. In Home Assistant: Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Local Calendar, starting data Import calendar file.
    3. In the Moin Hamburg options, add it to the calendars with Hamburg events.

    A keyword matches the start of an event title up to the end of a word, ignoring case; * stands for any text. "Dom: Winterdom" counts, "Domführung" does not. Change the keywords in the integration's options.

    Get it

    Moin Hamburg is a custom integration for Home Assistant 2026.9 or newer, on GitHub.

    1. Since Home Assistant 2026.11: Settings → Marketplace → Add from link, enter . Before: in HACS as a custom repository of type Integration.
    2. Restart, then add Moin Hamburg under Settings → Devices & services and pick your weather entity.
    3. Choose the theme moin-hamburg in your profile — or let the integration make it the default.
